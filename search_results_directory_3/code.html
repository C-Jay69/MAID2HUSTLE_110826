How our payment protection works

M2H

Updated 2 days ago

4 min read

At MAID 2 HUSTLE, your security is our top priority. We use a secure escrow-style payment system to ensure that both homeowners and service providers are protected throughout the entire booking process.

The Booking Process

When you book a service, your payment is processed immediately but held securely. We do not release the funds to the service provider until the job has been completed and marked as finished in the app.

  • Secure Checkout: All transactions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL technology.
  • Hold Period: Funds are safely held while the service is in progress.
  • Completion: Payment is released automatically 24 hours after service completion unless a dispute is filed.

Cancellation & Refunds

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both parties. If you cancel more than 24 hours before your scheduled service, you will receive a full refund minus a small processing fee.

info Pro Tip

Always communicate through the MAID 2 HUSTLE messaging system to ensure your interactions are documented in case of a dispute.

Dispute Resolution

In the rare event that a service does not meet the agreed-upon standards, you can open a dispute through the 'Bookings' tab. Our support team will review the photos and communication history to reach a fair resolution within 48 hours.
