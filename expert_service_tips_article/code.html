5 Secret Habits of Top-Rated Home Cleaners

Sarah Jenkins

Expert Contributor • Oct 24, 2023

Maintaining a spotless home isn't just about the tools you use; it's about the systems you put in place. In this guide, we dive deep into the professional workflows that save time and deliver pristine results every single time.

1. The Top-to-Bottom Rule

Professionals never start with the floors. Gravity is your friend and your enemy. By starting at the highest point in a room (ceiling fans, top shelves) and working downwards, you ensure that any falling dust is captured as you progress, preventing double work.

2. Micro-Zoning Strategy

Instead of seeing a kitchen as one big task, pros break it into 2-foot zones. Clean one zone completely—from cabinets to backsplash to counter—before moving your feet.

  • check_circle Always use high-grade microfiber cloths instead of paper towels.
  • check_circle Let cleaning solutions "dwell" for 5-10 minutes before wiping.
  • check_circle Carry all your tools in a wearable caddy to minimize trips.
